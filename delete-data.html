Basket Buddy – Delete My Data

This page explains how to request deletion of your personal data from the Basket Buddy app, developed by SuperApps.

To delete your data, follow these steps:
  1. Send an email to superapps.mb@gmail.com with the subject line:
    "Data Deletion Request – Basket Buddy"
  2. Include the email address linked to your Basket Buddy account (used for Google Sign-In).
  3. We will confirm receipt within 5 business days and complete deletion within 30 days.
Send deletion request

What gets deleted

  • Your account profile (name, email address)
  • All grocery list items and purchase history
  • Usage analytics linked to your account

What may be retained

  • Anonymised, non-identifiable aggregate statistics (e.g. total items scanned) with no link to your identity, retained for up to 90 days for service improvement.

For further questions, see our full Privacy Policy or email superapps.mb@gmail.com.
