Basket Buddy – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 May 2026

Basket Buddy ("we", "our", or "the app") is a family grocery-list assistant developed by SuperApps (superapps.mb@gmail.com). This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how it is protected.

1. Data We Collect

  • Account information — your name and email address collected via Google Sign-In, used solely to identify your family group.
  • Grocery list data — items, quantities, stores, and prices you enter or scan.
  • Receipt images — photos you choose to scan are processed by our backend to extract item data and are not stored permanently after processing.
  • Usage data — basic, anonymised analytics (feature usage frequency) to improve the app. No advertising identifiers are used.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To sync your grocery list across your family's devices.
  • To power the AI assistant that suggests items and tracks prices.
  • To send optional push notifications about list updates.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in Supabase (EU region) using row-level security so only authenticated members of your family group can access it. All data in transit is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Google Sign-In — for authentication (Google Privacy Policy).
  • Supabase — for database and auth (Supabase Privacy Policy).
  • Anthropic Claude API — receipt text and grocery queries are sent for AI processing; no personal identifiers are included in these requests.

5. Children's Privacy

Basket Buddy is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by emailing superapps.mb@gmail.com. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. The effective date at the top of the page will always reflect the latest version. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.

8. Contact

Questions? Email us at superapps.mb@gmail.com.
